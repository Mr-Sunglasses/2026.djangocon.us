Tobias McNulty is the co-founder and CEO of Caktus Group, a Django-focused consulting firm with nearly 20 years of service to the Python community. A member of the Django Software Foundation (DSF) Ops Team and co-author of the Django messages framework, Tobias specializes in scaling web applications and modernizing development workflows. He holds a Master of Public Health from UNC-Chapel Hill and is dedicated to building software that drives impact in health services, education research, international elections, and support for underserved communities.
