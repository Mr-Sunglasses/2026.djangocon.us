James (he/him) is a front-end developer focused on user-friendly solutions for complex problems. He is an open source maintainer on multiple polyfills, and has contributed significant features to Sass. He is a participant in the WebDX Community Group, and writes articles and makes videos exploring front end concepts.

When he isn't coding, James is caring for his 2 young kids, or attempting to identify nocturnal migrating birds that happen to call while flying over a microphone on his roof.
