Angufibo Lincoln is a software engineer based in Kampala, Uganda, focused on backend systems, performance engineering, and developer tooling.

He is the founder and maintainer of FlameIQ, an open-source tool that helps teams detect and prevent performance regressions in CI pipelines. His work centers on making performance a testable and enforceable part of the development workflow.

Lincoln has contributed to open-source projects within the Python ecosystem, including CPython and OpenTelemetry, and is passionate about building tools that improve reliability and developer experience.

He also leads Python Kampala, one of Uganda’s largest Python communities, where he organizes meetups, mentors developers, and supports the growth of the local tech ecosystem.

When he’s not writing code, he’s thinking about how to make software systems more efficient, scalable, and accessible.
