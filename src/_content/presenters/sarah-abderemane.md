Sarah Abderemane (sabderemane) is a software engineer and accessibility advocate in France.
She is the former Vice President of the Django Software Foundation and a member of the Django Accessibility team.
She organizes the Django Paris meetups and co-founded Djangonaut Space, a mentoring program aimed at helping people contribute to the Django ecosystem.
